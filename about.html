About the studio

Small by design. Personal by nature.

ABJ Design Co. is an independent San Diego studio creating thoughtful websites for businesses that care about how they show up online.

See the work Start a conversation
Aiden JajoDesigner, developer, and the person answering your emails.
The reason behind it

I built the kind of studio I would want to hire.

Clear communication, thoughtful design, careful code, and no maze of account managers between you and the person doing the work.

ABJ Design Co. exists for businesses that have outgrown the generic template, but do not need the overhead or distance of a large agency.

Every project is handled directly from the first conversation through launch. That makes the process more responsive, the decisions more intentional, and the finished site more personal to the business it represents.

The ABJ approach

Precision without the coldness.

The work is clean and disciplined, but never sterile. The goal is a website that feels refined, useful, and unmistakably yours.

What guides the work

A few principles behind every decision.

01 · Purpose

Built for the business, not the trend.

Design choices should make the business clearer, more credible, and easier to choose. Looking current matters. Chasing every trend does not.

02 · Clarity

Direct process. Clear expectations.

You know what is being built, what it costs, and what happens next. No vague scope, unnecessary layers, or disappearing acts.

03 · Ownership

Your website should remain yours.

Your domain, content, and finished site belong to you. The project should create independence, not lock you into a system you cannot leave.

The experience

One person, from the first idea to launch day.

01

Listen before designing.

We begin with the business, the audience, and what the website actually needs to accomplish. The visual direction comes after the purpose is clear.

02

Shape the experience.

Structure, copy, hierarchy, and visual language are worked through together so the site feels cohesive before development begins.

03

Build it carefully.

The site is developed responsively, refined across screen sizes, and tested with the same attention given to the visual concept.

04

Launch with confidence.

You receive a polished website, ownership of the final work, and a clear understanding of how the site can evolve after launch.
